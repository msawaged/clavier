Pick a song to learn it

Kiano can learn almost any song from a MIDI file (ends in .mid) — a small free music file that lists a song's notes.

  1. Search "[song name] piano midi" on BitMidi, FreeMidi or MuseScore.
  2. Download the .mid file.
  3. Tap ＋ Add your own song and pick it. If it has several parts, choose the melody, vocals or piano part.

Simple piano versions work best. Your file stays on your device — Kiano never uploads or shares it.
